# Philip Siniscalchi

**Founder of [SMARTHAUS](https://github.com/SmartHausGroup). Building AI you can prove.**

I spent 20 years selling and leading at enterprise scale — AWS L7 Principal, NTT Global, the rooms where billion-dollar AI deployments either work or quietly fail. Most of them fail. The reason is always the same: a stochastic system shipped without mathematical guardrails, defended with marketing language instead of proof.

SMARTHAUS is the company I built to answer that.

We ship deterministic AI infrastructure — runtime governance (**UCP**), deterministic inference (**SAID**), and the mathematical discipline that produces both (**Mathematical Autopsy**). Every claim our software makes is backed by a checkable artifact: a lemma, an invariant, a notebook proof, a signed receipt, or a CI gate. If a property can't be proven, it doesn't ship.

The bet: enterprise AI gets governed by math, or it doesn't get governed at all.
